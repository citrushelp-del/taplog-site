Taplog

Simple on-device baby logging. Your data stays on your device.

Taplog helps caregivers keep everyday care notes on the phone they already use. No account is required. Logs stay on that phone and are not synced. The one-time unlock is confirmed with Google Play on Android and the App Store on iPhone. That store must be reachable for the check. Care logs are not sent to the store. A family list, if you use one, stores names and codes on the same phone and does not copy the logs.
